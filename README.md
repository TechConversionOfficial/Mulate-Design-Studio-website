## About the business

- **What it does:** Mulate Design Studio is presented as an architecture and interior design practice. Its publicly advertised business activity also refers to renovation, turnkey execution and project-management consultancy (PMC).
- **Who it works with:** potential homeowners, housing societies, commercial-property owners and others seeking architectural planning, interior improvements or renovation support. These are the project types identified in a published Mulate recruitment advertisement, not a verified list of every completed client engagement.
- **Why its portfolio matters:** architectural and interior design decisions depend on how a space is planned, experienced and delivered. Prospective clients need a way to evaluate the studio's design direction before commissioning a project.
- **Why a website matters:** a credible digital portfolio gives the practice a place to explain its areas of work, present approved imagery and encourage a first consultation without relying entirely on third-party social posts.

## The problem

A design studio sells judgement and execution capability, rather than an off-the-shelf product. A prospective client wants to know what kinds of spaces the practice works on, how its design thinking translates to real projects and whether the studio could handle a particular brief.

For an architecture-and-interiors website, the challenge is to **put the work first without hiding the services or the enquiry route**. Large photographs need room to breathe, while visitors still need clear explanations of architecture, interior design, renovation and project coordination.

This is the business communication problem relevant to the portfolio listing. An audit of a previous Mulate website, a confirmed historical conversion problem and a dated redesign brief were not available; those things should not be assumed.

## How Tech Conversion presented the project

The [Tech Conversion portfolio](https://techconversion.tech/portfolio) describes Mulate Design Studio as an elegant architecture and interior design website showcasing thoughtful spaces, experience and end-to-end design expertise. The following points explain that **documented design direction**, rather than claiming unverified internal build steps.

1. **Let the practice's work carry the introduction.** Lead with authentic architecture and interior projects wherever approved project photography is available, rather than relying on general stock images.
2. **Make the disciplines legible.** Explain architecture, interiors, renovation and related project coordination clearly so visitors can identify the right kind of enquiry.
3. **Give projects useful context.** When the client provides source details, pair each portfolio image with its type, scope and status: completed work, concept or visualisation.
4. **Make the design process understandable.** Help clients see how an initial conversation, planning, concept development and execution coordination may fit together without inventing a fixed studio process.
5. **Provide a route to trust.** Saved website-component files include a seven-card Mulate video-testimonial layout, indicating testimonial presentation work was prepared. Its presence on the live production site was not independently confirmed.
6. **Make the next step obvious.** A prospective client should be able to find accurate contact information or an enquiry option without leaving the portfolio unexplained. Live form behaviour was not independently tested.
7. **Respect smaller screens.** Architectural photography and project navigation should remain readable on mobile. No independent mobile audit or performance report was available for this repository.

## Standards worth checking

These are **evaluation criteria**, not proof of independent certification or tests completed for the Mulate website.

- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): assess the effect of large galleries and imagery on real-user loading, interaction responsiveness and layout stability.
- [WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): review contrast, keyboard navigation, meaningful image alternatives, accessible links and enquiry forms.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): check crawlability, helpful service and portfolio content and appropriate indexing.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): evaluate accurate organisation, service and breadcrumb markup where actually implemented.
- [India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework](https://www.meity.gov.in/data-protection-framework): examine applicable handling of contact and project-enquiry information.
- **Portfolio integrity:** distinguish real completed spaces from photorealistic renders, staged illustrations and prospective designs.
